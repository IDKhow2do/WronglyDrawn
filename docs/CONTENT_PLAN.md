# Content plan: three levels, the Red Pen, 20 minutes each

Direction from the author (2026-11-12): three levels, the antagonist is the Red Pen, each level takes about 20 minutes.

## Story in three levels

| Level | Place | Sticker | Boss | What the Red Pen does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blind Plaza | Eye | The Eraser | Seen only as red marks: crosses on walls, a red line through the road. |
| 2 | Mute Harbor | Mouth | The Hush | Appears in the sky in cutscenes and crosses things out while you watch. |
| 3 | Limp Works (inside the Correction Tower) | Legs | The Red Pen | Fights you in person. |

- Levels 1 and 2 keep their bosses. Both become the Red Pen's servants, and each boss cutscene shows a red mark on it.
- Level 3 is the Legs chapter. The stickers finally have one level each.
- The ending resolves the title: the city stays drawn "wrong", and that is fine.

## Story direction (author, 2026-11-13): the Red Pen is won over, not defeated

- **Theme:** drawing it wrong is fine. Different is not the same as wrong.
- **The Red Pen** is a teacher's pen. It only knows how to correct, and it believes it is helping the city.
- **Contrast:** in levels 1 and 2, the more it corrects, the blinder and quieter the city gets. In level 3 the archive shows that the crossed-out drawings are children's drawings.
- **Its servants** (the Eraser, the Hush, the stationery creatures) are correction tools. Repainted, they stop correcting. The game must say this to the player in words or pictures.
- **Ending:** the player does not break the Red Pen. The last TAG lends it paint, and for the first time it draws instead of correcting. Warm, not triumphant.
- This replaces "Fights you in person ... the last hit is a TAG" as a defeat: the fight stays, its end changes meaning.
- **Refined (author, 2026-11-14):**
  - **Theme:** imagination must not be erased by the correct answer. As AI agents take over more of the making, imagination is the scarce part.
  - **The Red Pen** is the outside world's standard of judgement. It is not a teacher and the children did not draw it. It comes from outside the page.
  - **Its look:** exact, manufactured, metallic, ruler-straight. It is the one thing in the game that is not crooked, and it must look out of place. It gets no doodle parts.
  - **WAI:** the name sounds like 歪 (crooked: not the correct answer, but creative) and like WHY (dares to question).
  - The ending stays: won over, not destroyed. WAI's paint is the first imperfect thing to touch it.
- **Built (story pass, 2026-11-14):** intro book, title cards, level outros + celebration, the END with the letter, the Red Pen's margin slips. Outline, every line in en + zh, the three letter drafts: docs/story/NOTES.md; code: src/story/.

## Reaching 20 minutes per level

Today the playtest bot finishes level 1 in about 7 minutes and level 2 in about 8.

Each level gets the same four additions:
1. **A second district** behind the current one, opened by the second Guardian.
2. **A mid-boss** halfway through, guarding the third sticker use.
3. **Two or three new creatures**, each built around one player action.
4. **Side goals:** one optional challenge room and one hidden sticker per level.

## New creatures (first proposal)

| Level | Creature | Behaviour | Counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | White-out bottle | Rolls over your paint and blanks it | Spray it from behind |
| 1 | Stapler | Jumps and pins WAI in place | Dodge the jump, then TAG it |
| 2 | Megaphone crab | Steals your shout and fires it back | Shout when its shell is open |
| 2 | Fog jelly | Drifts and hides paint drops and paths | Lend an Eye to see through it |
| 3 | Stamp press | Slams "CORRECT" stamps on the floor | Run through with long Legs |
| 3 | Ruler snake | Straightens crooked platforms so they fall | Paint it to bend it back |
| 3 | Compass spinner | Draws red circles that hurt | Jump the line, hit its needle |

## Level 3 outline: Limp Works

- **Entry:** through the tower door that level 1 already opens.
- **Floors:** conveyor hall, stamping floor, the archive of crossed-out drawings, the roof.
- **Guardians:** three frozen machines that need Legs, then Eye and Mouth together.
- **Boss:** the Red Pen, three phases. It crosses out your stickers one at a time; the Eraser and the Hush return to help you; the last hit is a TAG.
