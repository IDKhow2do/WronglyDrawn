## Suggested order

1. **Clean up the screen** (items 2, 3 and 4, plus the HUD under menus in 5 and 13). This is all UI and fast, and every player sees it within the first minute.
2. **End-of-level celebration** (item 5).
3. **Camera** (item 6).
4. **Sound** (item 1). This is the biggest change, so it needs a decision first: free sound packs (for example Kenney or freesound.org with CC0 licences) or AI-generated sound.
5. **Art passes** (items 7, 10, 11 and 8), then phones (item 9).

## Progress

**2026-11-07, screen clean-up (items 2, 3 and 4, plus the HUD under menus in 5 and 13).** Screenshots after the change are in [polish-audit/after-cleanup/](polish-audit/after-cleanup/).
- **Loading:** the logo, a progress bar and one friendly line per step ("Sharpening the pencils…"). The engine checklist is gone.
- **Title:** one big logo with the subtitle, then START GAME and LEVELS. The fake HUD stickers (hearts, level tag, sticker cards) and "DEMO START" are gone. It also fits short phone screens.
- **First seconds:** the controls sheet starts folded into an "H · controls" chip. The area title shows first, and a short controls line follows once it has faded. The starting boon waits until WAI has moved about 12 m, or 30 s have passed.
- **HUD:** at most 2 toasts on screen, with the rest queued. Toasts are smaller, slide in near the top, and stay longer when they're long. The small HUD logo is gone, and the sticker cards fade to half opacity after 6 s without changes.
- **Menus:** pause, the completion screen and the boon picker hide the HUD underneath.
- Items 2, 3 and 4 should now be about a B. Item 5 still needs its own celebration pass.

**2026-11-08, end-of-level celebration (item 5).** Screenshots after the change are in [polish-audit/after-celebration/](polish-audit/after-celebration/).
- The camera glides from where you finished to a hero view of the woken level. Mute Harbor has its own higher view over the lagoon.
- Paint confetti falls. The title slams in, the stats count up one by one with ticks, and a rank stamp (S/A/B/C, with "MASTERPIECE!" and similar) thumps down.
- The rank comes from crowns found, smudges, hits taken and accuracy. Time is a record, never a penalty. Bests per level are saved, with NEW BEST tags or a "Best: S · 3:12" line.
- An UNLOCKED row lists the gear and capes earned this run, plus the next level. The usual unlock pop-up is skipped when it would land on the result.
- On phones it becomes a compact bottom card. In Chinese it is fully translated.

**2026-11-08, ENCORE stamping and cutscene camera.** Screenshots are in [polish-audit/after-encore/](polish-audit/after-encore/).
- ENCORE is no longer available from the start. It needs the **Golden Stamp**, a new Legendary boon (a gold card with a glow). It shows up in about 1 of 10 boon hands. The ENCORE-odds boon only appears once you have it.
- The decision window goes from 1.25 s to 2.4 s, and the countdown ticks speed up only in the last 0.8 s.
- Every TAG and stamp now shows a big cartoon rubber stamp that rises over the creature, then slams down and squashes. On a slip it skids off spinning.
- Each stamp that sticks gets an escalating banner, from STAMPED! and DOUBLE STAMP! up to LEGENDARY STAMP!!, with a numbered seal, the payout, sparks, and a rising chime from ×2.
- Cutscene camera: each shot is now planned once. It keeps the authored distance (at least 2.4 m), and if the spot is buried or blocked it swings around the subject or rises until the view is clear. The fountain close-ups used to sit 0.3 m above the floor, in the water and ground; they are now about 2 m up.

**2026-11-08, camera in tight spaces (item 6).** Before and after shots are in [polish-audit/after-camera/](polish-audit/after-camera/).
- **Lighthouse spiral stair (the "stares at the sky" case):** the camera used to collapse into WAI's head, as close as 0.5 m with only sky in view. The cause was a bug: the shoulder check kept 0.22 m from a wall, but the backward sweep uses a 0.28 m ball, so the sweep started "inside" the tower wall and reported no room. Now the clearances match. The shoulder offset slides in beside walls, and when squeezed and you aren't steering, the camera eases round behind WAI's direction of travel. Thin railings and balustrade panels no longer shove it. In a 600-frame walk without steering, the worst 10% of frames went from 0.5 m to 1.6 m (up) and from 0.6 m to 2.5 m (down). A player who steers sees no change.
- **Big creatures and the Eraser:** creatures with bigness ≥ 0.55 now block the camera. Their drawn bodies are much larger than their colliders, so the camera also casts a ray against their real meshes and stops short. Before, the camera could sit inside the Eraser (the whole view tinted by its skin); now it stays outside.
- **Props between the camera and WAI** fade to a ghost (26% opacity, ink outline hidden) and come back when clear. Ground pieces, merged building ink and merged dressing never fade. The merged rock and bush batches act as walls for the camera instead.
- **Grand stair** framing was already fine in both directions at normal pitch.
- **Cost:** about 0.015 ms per frame for the fader and creature rays. Frame time is unchanged at the 60 fps cap.
- **Still open:** walking up the spiral while hugging the tower without steering keeps the camera near 2 m (WAI visible, but close). In the road sweep (960 views), WAI was hidden in 2.7% of views, mostly by gorge walls and cliff edges, which are terrain and stay solid.

**2026-11-09, sound (item 1).**
- **Sound effects:** 51 of the game's sounds now play recorded CC0 samples from six Kenney packs, about 177 files and 1 MB in `public/audio/sfx/`.
  - Each play picks a random variation, and the same one never plays twice in a row.
  - Where the cartoon synth gives a sound its character (splats, hits, stamps, jumps), the synth plays quieter on top of the recording.
  - Sounds with no good recorded match stay synthesized: spray, roar, whoosh, shout and the Hush.
  - The synth is also the fallback while files load or if a file is missing.
  - Two new jingles: a fanfare when a level is complete and an unlock jingle for rewards.
- **Music:** five CC0 loops (about 5 MB in `public/audio/music/`) crossfade by mood.
  - Title: *Happy Lullaby*. Exploring: *Grasslands* in Blind Plaza and *Tropical Island* in Mute Harbor. Danger: *Insistent*. Combat: *Fast fight*.
  - The synth music only plays until the loops have loaded.
- **Fixed:** the title screen used to get the frozen session's "explore" mood, so title music never played.
- **Credits:** listed in `CREDITS.md` in each folder.
- **Not checked by ear:** the tracks were chosen from their descriptions, and test browsers are muted, so volumes and fit still need a listen.

**2026-11-09, playtest fixes (from the user's own play).**
- **Lost spray can:** a thrown can that lands out of reach comes back on its own. That means 2.2 m above or below WAI, more than 28 m away, or simply 4 s once out of reach. Any can left lying for 12 s also comes back ("CAN REDRAWN!"). Its beacon is now 14 m tall and shows through walls and fog.
- **Locked outside the boss arena:** the Eraser fight used to start at 9.5 m from the summit's centre while its wall ring stands at 9.2 m. Stepping onto the top stair locked WAI outside. Both bosses now start only once WAI is clearly inside (7.3 m), and WAI is pulled a step in if he's near the ring when it locks.
- **Clipping into the Eraser:** a box collider shaped like its eraser block now sits alongside the capsule. When it turns or lunges into WAI, he is pushed out. In a test pushing into all four sides, WAI stopped 3.0–3.7 m from its centre, outside its 2.1–2.7 m half-size. The Hush is left alone, because WAI must reach its core.
- **Intro pencil:** the graphite line used to draw on top of everything, even through the pencil. It now lies on the planks and is hidden behind the pencil and WAI. It is thinner, graphite-grey and lit. The pencil casts a shadow, rests its tip on the line, and leans away from the camera with a small sway as it draws.
- **Seams:** a floating-prop scan of both levels found the cupolas from the architecture kit 0.1–0.3 m above off-centre roof ridges. They now sit on the real ridge. No other floating props were found.
- **Bot runs after the fixes:** Blind Plaza's Eraser fight and level end, and all of Mute Harbor including the Hush. Both completed with no errors.
